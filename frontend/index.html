End-to-End Pipeline Architecture (README.md)

How the Computer Vision + Document AI system processes scanned bank documents step-by-step

1 Step 1 — Synthetic Data Generation

Generates 60 synthetic document images per class (480 total) with realistic bank layouts, headers, and fields.

python3 data/generate_synthetic_data.py --per_class 60
2 Step 2 — OpenCV Preprocessing

Performs FastNLMeans denoising, deskewing (rotation correction via minAreaRect), and auto-cropping to document boundary.

python3 src/data_preprocessing.py
3 Step 3 — CNN & Transfer Learning Training

Trains 4 neural backbones (Custom CNN, MobileNetV2, ResNet50, EfficientNetB0) with early checkpoint saving.

python3 -m src.train --model all --epochs 8
4 Step 4 — Evaluation & Comparison

Generates classification reports, confusion matrices, and model comparison JSON/Markdown benchmarks.

python3 -m src.evaluate
5 Step 5 — Grad-CAM Explainability

Generates activation heatmaps using forward/backward hooks on final conv layers for visual inspection.

python3 -m src.gradcam --image data/raw/cheque/cheque_0000.png --model resnet50
6 Step 6 — Phase 2 OCR Extraction

Uses Tesseract OCR on region-of-interest crops to extract Date, Payee, Amount, Account No, and IFSC.

python3 -m src.ocr_extraction --image data/raw/cheque/cheque_0000.png
